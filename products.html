PRODUCT 01

Master Cartons

100% recyclableEco-friendly corrugated packaging

Our master cartons are best-in-class corrugated shipping boxes, built for freight consolidation. They let you bundle multiple products into a single, sturdy outer pack that stands up to stacking, handling and long-distance transport.

Every carton is made to order: we tailor the dimensions, ply and paper quality to the weight, fragility and journey of your product.

  • Available in 3 ply, 5 ply and 7 ply construction
  • Narrow flute, B-flute and jumbo flute options
  • Custom dimensions and paper grades to your specification
  • 100% recyclable corrugated board for freight, export and bulk dispatch

PRODUCT 03

Inner Cartons

100% recyclableEco-friendly corrugated packaging

The inner carton is the box each individual item is packed in, while the master carton holds all of the inner cartons together. Well-made inner cartons protect each unit, simplify counting and make shipping and moving easier.

We craft inner cartons in any colour and design, for both household and industrial products.

  • Individual unit packaging that nests inside master cartons
  • Any colour and design specification
  • Suited to household and industrial products
  • Makes storage, counting and dispatch simpler

Duplex Boxes

100% recyclablePlastic-free alternative

Duplex boxes are available in a variety of designs, sizes, colours and finishes. They are easy to fold and store, yet durable and reliable in use.

Their smooth, printable surface makes them a natural choice for consumer and commercial packaging, from bottles to bakery products.

  • Multiple designs, sizes, colours and finishes
  • Easy to fold and store flat
  • Durable and dependable in use
  • Popular for bottles, bakery and retail products

PRODUCT 05

Honeycomb Cartons

100% recyclableEco-friendly corrugated packaging

Honeycomb cartons are sturdy boxes made from interconnected paper cells. This structure gives them very high compression strength, making them ideal for demanding industrial use.

Made with high-quality honeycomb panels, they are heavy-duty yet lightweight, precise and of industrial-grade quality.

  • Interlocking cell structure for high pressure strength
  • Heavy-duty performance at a low weight
  • Precision-made from quality honeycomb panels
  • Ideal for industrial and heavy goods

PRODUCT 06

Corrugated Sheets

Biodegradable kraft paperPlastic-free alternative100% recyclable

Corrugated paper sheets are used by packers and movers to wrap all kinds of products. The fluted texture absorbs knocks and protects surfaces from wear and tear during handling and transport.

They are a simple, economical way to add protection around furniture, equipment, glassware and more.

  • Fluted texture absorbs impact
  • Protects surfaces from scratches, wear and tear
  • Corrugated rolls and sheets in biodegradable kraft paper
  • A recyclable, plastic-free wrapping material
