Sustainability · Eco Promise

Our promise of sustainable packaging

Eco-friendly corrugated packaging is paper-based, 100% recyclable and a practical plastic-free alternative for shipping, retail and industrial packs. This is how we approach sustainability — as everyday material choices, not as a certification claim.

100% recyclable board

Master cartons, inner cartons, corrugated sheets, partitions and layer pads are made from paper fibre. After they have done their job, they can go into the paper recycling stream. We also encourage customers to reuse sturdy cartons in their own warehouses for as long as the boxes stay dry and square.

Biodegradable kraft paper

Kraft liners and corrugated wraps are a biodegradable, plastic-free way to cushion and protect goods. Used in place of plastic film or foam where the application allows, they keep the pack simple, strong and paper-based from the outside in.

A plastic-free alternative

For many food, grocery, e-commerce and industrial shipments, a well-designed corrugated carton already does the work of mixed plastic packing. Right-sizing the box, adding paper-based partitions and avoiding unnecessary fillers keeps the pack lighter and easier to recycle.

Eco-conscious materials

We favour eco-conscious materials and processes. That includes paper-based board as standard and, where the job allows, water-based inks. We do not claim formal eco certifications we do not hold; we simply specify materials carefully and help you choose a construction that uses no more board than the journey needs.

In practice

Small choices, cleaner packs

Tell us if you would like a recyclable, reusable or plastic-free specification. We can recommend a ply and flute that protects the product without over-boxing, and we can supply kraft wraps, corrugated rolls and paper-based inserts to match.

  • Right-size to cut wasted board
  • Paper-based pads and partitions
  • Reusable master cartons in-house
  • Flatten and recycle at end of life
