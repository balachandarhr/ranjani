Version 10 — client-supplied factory photography (own work, supplied by Ranjani Packaging; not stock)
Photographs of the Sattur Road unit: plant and machinery, raw materials and the finished-goods store.
Each image was gently enhanced (auto-contrast, slight warmth and saturation, light denoise and sharpening)
and cropped to a consistent 4:3 frame. No elements were added, and no certifications or grade specifications
were inferred from the photos.
- ranjani-stitching.webp: shop floor with a KARUNYA stitching machine, workers and carton stacks.
- ranjani-printed-sheets.webp: stacks of printed duplex and laminated sheets.
- ranjani-kraft-sheets.webp: a tall stack of kraft and board sheets. A printed ‘Platinum’ board in the
  original foreground was cropped out of the frame so third-party brand art is not shown.
- ranjani-finished-store.webp: finished flat corrugated box stacks in the store. The handwritten
  ‘Swasthick Skandha’ client tag was lightly blurred so the name is not readable.
- ranjani-biopolymer.webp: woven bags of bio-polymer grades. Supplier name, address, phone and email
  (Shri Shivam Bio Polymers, Salem) were blurred; the bio-polymer bag look and grade marking were kept.
- ranjani-printing-machine.webp: printing machine (flexo / rotary).
- ranjani-slotter.webp: slotter and creaser. Hanging clothes at the top of the original frame were cropped out.
- ranjani-pasting.webp: pasting and lamination section, with the team handling sheets.
- ranjani-generator.webp: SilentPower diesel generator for power backup. Scrap at the edges of the
  original frame was cropped out.
- ranjani-corrugation.webp: corrugation line with kraft reels and an operator (used on the home teaser).
- ranjani-corrugation-line.webp: the same corrugation line without the operator (plant card, page hero,
  About plant preview and gallery).
