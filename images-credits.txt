  backdrop with a ground shadow. Box print unchanged.
- ranjani-spnka-side.webp, ranjani-spnka-front-photo.webp (v4): the same carton at Ranjani's unit; cropped (date stamp removed),
  exposure / white balance / sharpening adjusted.
