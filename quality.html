Material Grades & Plies

The right strength for the journey ahead

We make 3, 5 and 7 ply cartons in narrow, B and jumbo flute, and we tailor paper quality to your product. These are the constructions we work with every day.

3, 5 and 7 ply

3 ply suits lighter products and shorter journeys. 5 ply is the all-rounder for medium-weight goods. 7 ply is for heavy or export loads that must survive tall stacks and rougher handling.

Narrow, B and jumbo flute

Narrow flutes give a smooth print face. B-flute balances cushioning with a flatter surface for graphics. Larger (jumbo) flutes add cushioning and vertical stacking strength.

Paper quality, made to order

Design, dimensions and paper quality are fully customisable. Tell us the product weight and how high boxes will be stacked, and we will recommend the right construction.

Board close-up

The structure behind the strength

Flute profile and ply count decide how a carton cushions, stacks and takes print. We work with eco-conscious, paper-based board grades and will recommend a construction that protects the product without using more material than the journey needs.

Want us to recommend a ply and flute?

Share the product weight, stack height and shipping route and we will suggest a construction.
