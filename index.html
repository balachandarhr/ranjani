Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our products, customisation and how to place an enquiry.

Still have a question?
What packaging products does Ranjani Packaging make?
We make master cartons (3, 5 and 7 ply), printed master cartons, inner cartons, duplex boxes, honeycomb cartons, corrugated sheets, along with pallet boxes, partitions and layer pads.
Can you make boxes in custom sizes and designs?
Yes. Design, dimensions, ply, flute and paper quality can all be customised to your product and shipping needs.
What details do you need to give me a quote?
Share the internal box dimensions (L × W × H), product weight, quantity, preferred ply or flute (if known), printing requirements and delivery location. Photos or samples of the product also help.
How do I know whether I need 3, 5 or 7 ply?
It depends on product weight, how high the boxes will be stacked and how far they travel. Tell us these details and we’ll recommend the right construction. Our Packaging Tips page also explains the basics.
Do you print logos and product information on cartons?
Yes. We print master and inner cartons in your colours and artwork, including logos, product details and handling symbols.
Which industries do you serve?
We supply packaging for fireworks and crackers (the home industry of Sivakasi), automobiles, processed food, fruits and vegetables, heavy-duty industrial goods, electronics, pharma and healthcare, and many other sectors.
